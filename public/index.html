What the model saw

Three numbers, one estimate

01

Detect

A YOLOv10 model scans each photo and boxes every car, truck, bus and motorcycle it finds.

02

Average

Counts from up to five photos are averaged, giving you a stable footfall figure instead of a one-shot guess.

03

Multiply

Average footfall × the midpoint of your low and high sale per car = a rough estimate of the day's revenue.

Rough analysis only. It does not model dwell time, hourly turnover, seasonality, or vehicles that park for free. Use it as a directional signal, not accounting.
